THE ROBOT LIBRARY · IN DEVELOPMENT

Keep the experience.
Choose the next chapter.

The planned library gives you one active robot and a collection of saved minds. Start with a blank template, build on a saved robot, or import a .pipmind file.

01

Save a copy.

Keep the robot's rules, guides and learned lessons in a portable package of Markdown files. Favorite a save to protect a starting point you want to keep.

02

Explore. Then return.

Rolling autosaves preserve the mind before learning changes it. Load an earlier save if its direction stops making sense; older unprotected autosaves expire.

03

Bring a different mind.

A saved robot is a starting point for a separate active copy. Loading it changes the decision maker while the account keeps its trades, positions and history.

A save restores the mind.

It cannot undo a trade, recover a loss, or rewind an account. The same saved experience also does not guarantee the same future decisions.

GUIDANCE & AUTONOMY · THE DESIGN

A trader that learns.
An owner with a voice.

01 / OWNER CHAT

Ask why. Suggest a change.

The planned private chat lets you explore a decision's recorded reasons and offer guidance. Direct edits to its decision-making Markdown require your confirmation of the proposed changes.

02 / AUTO LEARN

Let experience shape it.

With Auto Learn on, the robot can update its own rules through autonomous review. Turn it off to preserve its learned rules and guides while observations and decisions continue. Nightly learning is planned.

03 / ACCOUNTABILITY

Keep the reasons attached.

Owner guidance is another influence on the mind; it does not replace autonomous learning. The planned public record will keep decisions, outcomes and playbook changes together, including losses.

The model chooses its strategy. Independent code is designed to enforce account risk limits, reconcile positions and provide a kill switch. A saved mind cannot grant trading permission or reset those limits.
